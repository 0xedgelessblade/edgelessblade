# I'm Increasingly Convinced Tesla and SpaceX Will Merge

**Date:** 2026-10-14

---

I'm more and more convinced
Tesla and SpaceX will merge in the end
Put a few recent pieces of news side by side and it really is getting more and more interesting

On October 8, SpaceX announced it had signed a deal to acquire a nationwide 800 MHz spectrum portfolio

SpaceX was very clear: Starlink Mobile will combine satellites in space with terrestrial networks to fill in coverage, indoors included

Reading that far, some people's first thought is: "So how much is SpaceX going to spend building cell towers next?"
My first thought, though, was:
Cybercab

Because on Tesla's earnings call this July, Musk had already talked about two things:
One, integrating Starlink into Cybercab to fix patchy mobile coverage.
Two, the Starlink terminals in Tesla cars could potentially serve as a terrestrial relay for nearby phone or Wi-Fi users

In other words, "the car provides internet on the side" is a direction Musk himself has already floated.
What comes next is the business logic I find most interesting

For a telecom company, more coverage usually means another round of spending on equipment, power, sites and maintenance

But for a Cybercab fleet, the cars have to be bought anyway, they already have power, they're already on the road, and they already need a stable connection to operate
Suppose that someday these cars could also provide connectivity to people nearby:
the same car would get a shot at splitting its costs across two services, transport and telecom

Especially at big events
We've all been there: the concert lets out, everyone's packed shoulder to shoulder, phones just keep spinning, even hailing a ride freezes
Traditional carriers might have to send in extra cell-on-wheels trucks for backup
If Cybercab could double as a comms node in the future, the picture gets pretty funny:

Everyone else's backup trucks drive out there specifically to patch the signal
Cybercab gets paid a fare to go pick people up, and brings the comms gear to the scene while it's at it🤣
Crowds create ride demand, and ride demand brings the cars right to the crowds
One trip, a shot at two businesses

So I see SpaceX's spectrum purchase as a puzzle piece that could also serve Tesla's fleet in the future
On one side: satellites, spectrum and a communications network
On the other: vehicles, batteries, and a self-driving fleet that may keep growing

Cooperation doesn't have to mean a merger, I know that
But my read is that the business case for a merger keeps growing

Last of all, really, don't underestimate Cybercab
The biggest advantage of a silicon-based vehicle is
redeployment costs so low it's outrageous
Wherever you send the fleet, the fleet goes
As long as regulations don't stand in the way
someday when a city hosts a big event, it could even pull in Cybercabs from the major cities within 100 to 200 kilometers around
