# Which Floor You Live On

**Date:** 2026-07-25

---

When you live on the second floor
you trade insults with people on the street
When you live on the fifth floor
you can hear passersby cursing you
When you live on the tenth floor
you think the people on the street are waving hello
When you live on the 100th floor
you go: Say what?
I don't see any pedestrians
You're asking where I live?
I live on the ground floor🤣
In all my years out in the world I've rarely argued with anyone
Arguing with someone means:
"You expected others to receive you with a higher standard of treatment"
"But it didn't happen"
But in my world
if we run into each other
it means we're still on the same floor
If we're no longer on the same floor
we naturally won't meet again
