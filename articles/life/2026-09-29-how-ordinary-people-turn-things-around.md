**Date:** 2026-09-29

How does an ordinary person turn things around?

When a bear market comes,
dare to bet.
That's it.

A big bear
comes three times in ten years.

The atmosphere the market and the media create is:
"You want to get in? Are you crazy?"

So all the trading practice before that
is there to make you trust your own judgment,
not to dance to the tune of the market and the media.

Whatever school of trading you belong to,
the precondition for success is believing in yourself.
