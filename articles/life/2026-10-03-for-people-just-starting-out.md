# For People Just Starting Out

**Date:** 2026-10-03

---

For people whose lives are just starting out and who are still lost.

I have never worried about competition from technology.
After cars came along,
horse carriages became more expensive.
After quartz watches came along,
mechanical watches became more expensive.
After Muse / grok bot came along,
someone like me, who has always replied by hand on Threads,
will still have people who love that.

Cutting out a niche for yourself is far too easy. Please keep going deeper.

Conclusion: the market is enormous.
Slice niches any way you like
and you can still cut out a territory of your own.

The way to make yourself the top isn't to work hard at becoming the top.
It's to keep narrowing the niche
until you become the top of that limited space.

The algorithm is very smart and also very dumb,
but it can absolutely be shaped.
Post steadily and mold your account's labels.
Within your own edge,
become the top.

Step outside your edge
for the sake of a bigger audience,
and you'll end up lost in the crowd.

The advantage of being an individual is this:
no matter how small the niche, the people already in it
are more than you could ever finish serving.
