## Company Overview

BlackSky is a real-time space intelligence company that combines satellite imagery with AI analytics to deliver near-real-time geospatial intelligence. Unlike Planet (daily coverage), BlackSky focuses on "on-demand, real-time, high-resolution" intelligence services — think of it as "a real-time reconnaissance plane, in space."

- **Market cap tier**: Small cap
- **Exchange**: NYSE: BKSY
- **Headquarters**: Virginia, USA

---

## Why It Has Dominance in AI Real-Time Data

### Core Logic

```
on-demand satellite tasking → AI-enabled detection → real-time intelligence feed
```

1. **Real-time intelligence, not static imagery**: BlackSky isn't selling "photos," it's selling "intelligence" — AI automatically detects ships, aircraft and vehicles and outputs actionable intelligence directly
2. **The Gen-3 satellite revolution**: 35 cm ultra-high resolution, paired with AI analytics, takes the whole pipeline from capture to intelligence output in a matter of minutes
3. **Deep defense roots**: Defense and intelligence agencies are core customers, and the contracts are extremely sticky (an eight-figure international contract already signed)
4. **sensor-to-decision pipeline**: BlackSky's full pipeline covers everything from sensing to decision-making; it's not just a data vendor

### Dominance Assessment

～ In the niche of "real-time geospatial intelligence," BlackSky may be the listed company closest to being an "AI-native intelligence company." It doesn't just take pictures; it sells AI-generated intelligence directly — a higher-value data layer.

## Catalysts

1. **Full commercialization of Gen-3 satellites**: 35 cm resolution + AI analytics, with defense and commercial demand taking off together
2. **International expansion**: The eight-figure "sovereign space intelligence" contract model can be replicated across many countries
3. **Growing AI defense budgets**: US + allied spending on space intelligence is in a structural uptrend
4. **EBITDA about to turn positive**: The 2026 guidance of $6M-$18M in positive EBITDA shows the business model works

## Risks

1. **Customer concentration**: The US government accounts for a large share of revenue, so policy shifts are a big risk
2. **Small-cap volatility**: Small market cap, violent share-price swings
3. **Satellite race**: Planet, Maxar (acquired by Advent) and Capella Space are all fighting for turf
4. **Geopolitical risk**: International contracts are at the mercy of diplomatic relations
