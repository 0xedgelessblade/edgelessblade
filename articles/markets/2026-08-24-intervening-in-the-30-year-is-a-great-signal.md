**Date:** 2026-08-24

This intervention in the 30-year is a great signal

The Treasury put has been tested and found

the future isn't scary anymore
Long-end rates are the anchor for asset pricing
With an anchor, risk assets
can be priced accurately

Stock market caps can be very high
but the market is genuinely short on cash
That shows up in the 30-year Treasury yield climbing and climbing

And the market extrapolates linearly
it may go even higher from here

That put pressure on assets that don't generate cash flow
like gold and Bitcoin
and left negative-cash-flow companies out of favor

This trend has been running for a long time

Now the Treasury has finally stepped out and drawn a red line on rates
Rates don't even need to come down much
As long as there's a guarantee they won't go any higher
that guarantee alone is enough for assets to reprice

Because it cuts off
the possibility of the market continuing to extrapolate linearly
In the end, the only investing questions left are
do you trust the Treasury to hold the red line?
will the Fed play along with the Treasury?

Because if the long end doubles its buybacks
the short end has to double its issuance
And as for short-term bills, beyond the existing buyers
who are the new incremental buyers?
That's exactly what makes the GENIUS Act genius
