# The Cost of Every Kind of Leverage, Sorted Out Once and for All

**Date:** 2026-09-11

---

Sorting out the cost of every kind of leverage, once and for all
All leverage comes at a cost
And the cheaper the method
the more it hides a downside of its own
- Loans of every kind
Nobody takes your umbrella back when it starts to rain, and the rates are the lowest
Downside: capped amounts, never enough
- Margin in a brokerage account
The rate is a bit higher
but the interest is still very cheap
Downside: they take the umbrella back the moment it rains; when your maintenance ratio falls short, a sword hangs over your entire account
- Buying calls
High cost
Upside: your maximum risk is locked in from the start
- 2x ETFs
A bit of fund management fee on top of what buying calls costs
Upsides
Same thing, your maximum loss is locked in
Plus it saves brainpower
