# When AI Does the Price-Comparing, Hassle Stops Being a Moat

**Date:** 2026-10-02

---

The more I use Muse, the more unsettling it gets when I think it through
Comparing prices now takes a single sentence
Everyday goods, phone bills, insurance, hotels, flights
Say one sentence, and AI goes off and compares prices for you
And it does it fast and well
There are plenty of examples online already
I don't even need to share any

For investing, this hands us a frightening fact
Companies that treat hassle as pricing power
are going to see their gross margins eroded fast
Hassle is a product
Phone plans, utility rates, insurance and internet bandwidth: these bills all look like they're selling a service

But half the profit actually comes from:

You never finish comparing prices

A twelve-month promotion, and you didn't do the math on month thirteen, you forgot, and the company's margins shoot up
Hassle isn't a side effect of bad service
Hassle is part of the pricing
I call this advantage the hassle factor
The product can be ordinary
People stay because replacing it is more exhausting than putting up with it
That's the company's moat
So what can Muse and Grokbot do now?
They can scan your email for duplicate subscriptions, ask you, then cancel them; they can also open web pages, fill in forms, and negotiate on your behalf
CNBC cites Stanford researchers' estimate: consumer inertia, plus the friction of cancelling, can roughly double a seller's revenue
Businesses far too often make people go round and round through procedures just to cancel one subscription
And now that moat is being torn down fast
Phone bills won't disappear, and neither will water bills
But profit margins will be squeezed out fast
Take a good hard look at the stocks you hold whose moat is hassle
