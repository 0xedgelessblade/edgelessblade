# Stop Measuring Your Assets by Your Monthly Salary

**Date:** 2026-08-25

---

People who love investing
usually have a pretty solid mindset

But their families don't necessarily
I think the source of stress is often family

Especially after kids come along
your partner may get more anxious
and need more of a sense of security

But I think false security is a cardinal sin in investing
It pushes people to choose clearly worse holdings
just because they can't stomach the volatility

And yet beta
is precisely the key to compounding

The key to compounding is to bear the beta you can bear
and then pick the highest within that range

False security includes things like
why does someone whose daily swings already exceed their monthly salary still put all their focus on work?
The reason: false security

Do you really work hard for a whole month just to cover one day of what you lose in the stock market?

You can keep working
but your focus should be on reviewing your assets
You shouldn't keep measuring your assets with your monthly salary
Instead, use the daily up-and-down numbers in your account to measure how unimportant this job is. People often lament how much of their assets they've lost in terms of monthly salary
Wrong
You should use "the size of the swing" to measure how unimportant your job is
then turn back and put real effort into accepting the "normal" volatility of the investment market

When you use "the wrong ruler: your monthly salary" to measure stock market swings, all you're doing is magnifying your own anxiety
