Harvard slashes its Bitcoin position

In its 2025 Q3 13F filing, Harvard Management Company sharply increased its stake in the Bitcoin ETF (IBIT). The mood was fantastic back then: congratulations, Bitcoin had officially entered mainstream institutional asset allocation

But according to the latest 2026 Q1 disclosures
the IBIT holding fell from 5.35 million shares the previous quarter to about 3.04 million, a cut of roughly 43% in a single quarter. In the same quarter, the Ethereum ETF position it had built was closed out entirely

Nor were there any notable new positions in other crypto plays like MSTR, COIN, CRCL or STRC

The interesting part, though
is that even after the trim, IBIT is still the largest position among Harvard Management's disclosed holdings

The current top five holdings are roughly:
1. IBIT: about 12.8%
2. Alphabet Inc.: about 12.2%
3. SPDR Gold Shares (GLD): about 11.9%
4. Microsoft: about 11.4%
5. Booking Holdings: about 8.7%
TSM's weighting has also kept climbing, and it's now close to core-holding territory

For institutions, Bitcoin may be entering its next stage: an asset class that can be held long term
but whose weighting and volatility have to be kept in check
