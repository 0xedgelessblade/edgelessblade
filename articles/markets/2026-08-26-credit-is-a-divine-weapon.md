# Credit Is a Divine Weapon

**Date:** 2026-08-26

---

Long-term investing isn't reserved for people who've already made it

There's a divine weapon only people who still have a job get to wield:
the seven-year personal loan
It hands you your future wealth in an instant

That's a very big number
Use it well
and it's like having 22 of you making money in the stock market

So when should you take out the loan?
When the market is jumping at every rustle
seeing enemies in every shadow
when the troops are routed and scattering
when there's not a soul in sight
bravely take the loan and come in
Pick a US broad-market index ETF
and within 1 to 2 years it's sure to bear big fruit

Bears aren't hard to wait for
In any ten years of US stocks
you'll run into a big bear about 2 to 3 times
A small bear, a pullback within the year, comes around about once a year
None of these are hard to wait for
The hard part is: when the bear shows up, do you dare go in?

And taking the loan at other times?
You're on your own; live or die, it's fate
I've seen it go very well
and I've seen it go very badly

Taking the loan a bit later isn't wasted time
Trading a smaller sum first
is getting to know yourself
is getting to know the market
learning that strange things happen in the market all the time
The ticker nobody cared about last month
is suddenly back in bloom this month

Before that, are you really ready to handle such a big sum?

The daily swing on a seven-year loan's worth of money
can easily top your monthly salary
Some people see a day's gain or loss equal to their monthly pay
and the forced errors start right away
