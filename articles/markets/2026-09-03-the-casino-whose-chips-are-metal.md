# The Casino Whose Chips Are Metal

**Date:** 2026-09-03

---

There's a casino where a retail player's odds of winning any single round are only 49%

In other words, as long as the sample is long enough and you place enough bets, under negative expected value and the law of large numbers, the house edge will cash in sooner or later

So of course the casino wants you to raise your turnover: the longer you play and the more often you bet, the easier it is for it to turn that 1% statistical edge into money

But here's the catch
this casino's chips are made of "a certain precious metal"

So what's our strategy?
Exchange for chips, then don't play a single hand.
Don't take part in a negative-EV game, don't provide any turnover, just take the chips home and leave them on the shelf

That's taking only Asset Exposure and refusing to play the Casino Game

Whether "a certain precious metal" goes up in the end, I don't know

But one thing I'm sure of:
when the casino watches you walk off with its chips
without contributing a cent of Expected Loss,
it must be pissed as hell

Of course, the card-counting pros should keep playing; everyone has their own way of winning. What I'm talking about here is the average

Oh, by the way, this casino is called the US stock market
And the chips? Think of them as broad-market index ETFs
