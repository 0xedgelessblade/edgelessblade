# Investing Is Like Playing Baseball

**Date:** 2026-10-13

---

Investing is like playing baseball
and the kind with especially simple rules

Watch three good pitches go by, and you're not out
Even stand there and watch 100 good pitches, and you're still not out

But swing and miss three times, and you really are out

The market offers chances every day,
but you don't need to take every one of them

The most important skill in investing isn't just knowing when to swing, it's knowing that when you can't read the pitch, you don't need to swing at all

Be patient inside your own edge
and wait for the market's worst mistake pitch
