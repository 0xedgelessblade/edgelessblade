# Find the Camp That's Your Calling

**Date:** 2026-09-17

---

The longer the discussion goes on, the more you notice
that long-term and short-term investors
often just aren't on the same wavelength

I've seen excellent examples on both sides
For any one person
rather than arguing over which camp has the edge
what you should really do
is figure out which camp is your true calling

The simplest way
is to split your accounts
and try a different school of investing in each

Give each account
at least three years to go through a full cycle
and let each account's performance speak for itself

Don't mourn the three years the other accounts "wasted"
Once you find the way of trading that suits you
short-term or long-term
growing your wealth becomes as natural as eating and drinking
