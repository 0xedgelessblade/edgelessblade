# Saylor Says He'll Sell BTC. Bitcoin Shrugs.

**Date:** 2026-05-06

---

Skip the news, and you lose touch with reality
Believe the news, and you lose touch with the facts

The line going viral across crypto today: "Michael Saylor is getting ready to sell BTC"

But did you notice something even stranger? Bitcoin barely reacted

Here's what the news won't tell you:

On yesterday's Q1 earnings call, Saylor said it himself: "We will probably sell some bitcoin to pay a dividend, just to inoculate the market."

The headlines were everywhere, yet bitcoin just wobbled around 80K, nothing like the reaction you'd expect to "the biggest corporate HODLer is dumping"

Why?
Because this was already hashed out once before

1. This isn't the first time they've been "almost out of cash"

In bitcoin's slide into the 60Ks a few months ago, as it fell back from its highs, MicroStrategy already faced very similar pressure:

BTC pulled back, mNAV was squeezed to around 1x, and the issue-shares-to-buy-coins flywheel lost efficiency fast

Cash flow got tight, unrealized losses were huge, and the market started whispering, "Will they be forced to sell coins?"

What happened next?
Not a blowup, but this: they scrambled to build up their cash reserves and forced the short-term FUD of "will MicroStrategy be forced to sell" back down

This round of "we might sell some coins" is really a sequel to last time's story, not a new script.

2. mNAV stuck at 1x, the old flywheel is broken

So far this year, MicroStrategy's mNAV has been moving sideways around 1x:

Share price ≈ the underlying BTC net value, meaning the market isn't willing to pay much of a premium anymore.

Under these conditions, issuing new shares through the ATM to buy BTC barely avoids hurting existing shareholders; the 2024 thrill of "issue shares and fatten BTC per share" is long gone

So over these months, with prices at 60,000-80,000, how could MicroStrategy still buy the bitcoin dip so aggressively?
Because they swapped in a new engine: STRC

3. STRC: the real play behind the 11% yield

What is STRC? Put simply, it's a perpetual preferred stock paying roughly 11% a year. MicroStrategy uses it to borrow from traditional credit markets and buys more BTC with the money.

The structure hinges on two points:

Lever up hard to buy BTC when it's cheap
When the market wrongly dumps BTC and mNAV can't get off the floor, sweeping up coins at a fixed 11% cost can still be positive carry for them:

They believe BTC's long-term annualized return can run at 20%+,

Pay investors 11%, and the spread in between is the shareholders' alpha. Selling some coins near the highs is actually a reasonable move
