# Options: Many Plays, Same Premise

**Date:** 2026-08-23

---

There are many ways to play options
but none of them can turn a loss into a profit

Options don't make risk disappear
they move the risk to the spot you set
and make it hit harder

The old saying "excel at stocks, move on to futures; excel at futures, move on to options" has its reasons

If picking a stock's direction, long or short, is a true-or-false question
then options are an essay question

Doing poorly on true-or-false and switching to short-answer
isn't out of the question either
Some people are just especially good at short-answer🤯

I've traded options for many years myself
For me, the one premise that has never changed is
I only sell a put
when I genuinely want to take the shares at that level
I only sell a call
when I'm genuinely willing to sell the shares at that level
Covered calls should also take IV rank into account
When grain is so cheap it hurts the farmer, don't sell calls😞
